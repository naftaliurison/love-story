Chapter One

The Day I Noticed You

South Africa • 2016

It was an ordinary school day. I was in Grade 11, skipping class — as one does — and I wandered over to sit with Dalya. And there you were, sitting right there with us, and all I could see were those beautiful green eyes.

I was quietly, secretly captivated from that moment. But we were in different circles, different grades, and we just never really crossed paths. Life carried on, but I never forgot those eyes.

For years after, we lived in the quiet margins of each other’s lives. A “Happy Birthday” message on Instagram. A small ritual, once a year, to remind the other person: I remember you. Neither of us knew that those tiny messages were threads slowly pulling us closer.

Chapter Two

Falling

2020 – 2022 • South Africa

The world stopped. But somehow, we started.

We bumped into each other on a Dije Shabbaton, and just like that we started talking again. It was natural, effortless — like picking up a conversation we’d been waiting years to have. But the world was still locked down. So we improvised.

Our first date: watching Tarzan at your grandparents’ house while they were out. We sat on blankets on the floor, and Fluffy came to sit right between us. You wanted me to hold your hand. I had no idea.

(I’m sorry. I’ve been making up for it ever since.)

Our second date in my Suzuki outside Hello Love, sipping iced coffees, talking about our favourite dogs (Golden Retrievers, ahem!) and who knows what. The world was closed, but ours was just opening up.

Iced coffees at Hello Love

After that, we were inseparable.

Walking hand-in-hand through Huddle Park. Getting lost in the beauty of Walter Sisulu Botanical Gardens. The zoo. Movie nights curled up together. And Michelos Pizzeria — where you would always, without fail, order the Creamy Neopolitana pasta and then feel sick afterwards. Every. Single. Time.

I loved every moment of it.

Chapter Three

Oceans Between Us

October 2022 – 2025

Then came the hardest chapter.

At the end of 2022, we both moved away to different countries. An entire world stretched between us, held together by a plan that required more patience than we knew we had: wait for your permanent residency, so you’d always be able to visit your family.

The distance was brutal. Time zones that never quite aligned. Video calls that were never long enough. Airports that became the most emotional places on earth — every arrival a celebration, every departure a quiet heartbreak.

But we held on. We visited each other whenever we could — we both flew back to South Africa for Eitan and Kayla’s wedding, and you came to visit me in Israel multiple times.

Eitan & Kayla’s wedding, South Africa
Reunited in Israel

We chose each other, again and again, across oceans and time zones and exhaustion. Two and a half long years of waiting. And we made it.

Some love is strong enough to cross any distance.
Chapter Four

Forever Starts at Sunset

2 July 2023 • Ramon Crater, Israel

In the Ramon Crater in southern Israel, as the desert sun painted the sky in gold and amber, I asked you to marry me. I was so excited, so happy, so deeply grateful to be able to ask my soulmate to spend forever with me.

I couldn’t keep the secret, of course. I never can with you. I basically told you every detail beforehand. But none of that mattered — the moment was still ours, and it was still perfect.

The vastness of the crater. The warmth of the desert sunset. And the rest of our lives stretching out ahead, wide open and full of promise.

And you? You just took the ring and put it on. You never even said yes.

But I knew.

Chapter Eight

Home, Fur, and Forever

2026 • Herzliya, Israel

Some chapters in a love story announce themselves loudly.

The proposal at sunset. The wedding day. The flights across oceans. The moments where life changes all at once.

And then there are chapters that become meaningful in quieter ways.

A new beginning. A new set of keys. A new rhythm. A home that slowly becomes more us with every ordinary day inside it.

This year, Tay, we stepped into another apartment together. Not our first place, but a new and beautiful chapter in the life we are building.

This home feels different. More spacious. More modern. More light and airy. More comfortable in all the ways that matter. Nice bathrooms too...

But what makes it special is not just the apartment itself. It is what you have done with it. You have taken the space and filled it with warmth, taste, care, and all the little touches that make it feel like us. Somehow, you have turned rooms and furniture into something much more meaningful — a real home.

A place that feels calm when we walk in.
A place that feels safe.
A place that feels like ours.
A place that others now call the Urison home.

And more than anything, it is about the way you make me feel at home. Because home is not only the apartment, or the furniture, or the beautiful space you have created. It is your hugs after a long day. It is your cuddles when everything feels heavy. It is the way being close to you can make the world feel softer, quieter, and safer.

I know this last month has been really tough. Not just for me, but for you too. And through all of it, you have been amazing. Patient, loving, strong, and there for me in ways I will never take for granted.

I would not ask for anyone else by my side. Not through the easy chapters, and definitely not through the difficult ones.

You are my comfort, my safe place, and my home.

And, of course, no home is complete without a first child.

Enter: Moptimus Prime.

Our brave, loyal, slightly disabled Robot Vacuum Mopper. The first official dependent of the Urison household. Tirelessly patrolling the floors, occasionally getting stuck, sometimes making questionable decisions, but always trying his best.

Moptimus Prime — always on duty

In many ways, he was the perfect first test of our parenting instincts: patience, supervision, rescue missions, and the ability to celebrate very small achievements.

But then came the real arrival. The one we had been waiting for.

Axel.

Our beautiful, gorgeous, majestic German Shepherd — perhaps slightly on the smaller side, but never short on personality, loyalty, or love. After so much waiting, planning, paperwork, and hoping, Axel finally made aliyah. And just like that, another piece of your heart arrived home.

Dog mum Tay

Seeing you with him has been one of the sweetest parts of this chapter. The way your face lights up around him. The way he follows you, trusts you, and knows you are his person. The way our home changed the moment he walked into it.

Suddenly, there were paws on the floor, fur on everything — and I really mean everywhere and everything, poor Moptimus — and a whole new kind of warmth and joy in the apartment.

Tayla & Axel — beach sunset

He did not just arrive in Israel. He arrived in our family.

And somehow, with you, me, Moptimus, and Axel, this home of ours feels fuller, funnier, softer, and more complete.

And this chapter became even more special because it was not only our home that grew warmer.

Your family moved to Israel too. After so much distance, so many flights, and so many goodbyes, having them closer has been such a beautiful part of this season. Another piece of home arriving here. Another reminder that the life we are building is becoming fuller in ways we once only hoped for.

And then Jomp got married. Josh married the beautiful Tamar, who has now joined this wonderful family. Watching your family grow, celebrate, and build new beginnings here has made this chapter feel even richer.

More people to love. More simchas to celebrate. More family around us. More home.

Tay, on your birthday, I keep thinking about how lucky I am to keep meeting new versions of life with you. Girlfriend Tay. Long-distance Tay. Fiancée Tay. Wife Tay. Home-making Tay. Dog-mum Tay. Each version more beautiful than the last.

You have crossed oceans. You have built a life in a new country. You have turned unfamiliar places into home. And now, watching you step into this new chapter with so much love, strength, grace, and joy, I am reminded again and again that I married someone truly extraordinary.

This chapter may not have started with fireworks or a sunset over the crater — although Iran did try to change that.

It started with keys, boxes, a robot mop, and one very special German Shepherd.

But maybe that is what makes it so beautiful. Because this is what forever really looks like.

A home we keep building.
A wife I keep falling for.
A dog who has finally come home.
A mildly dramatic robot vacuum.
And a life I would choose again every single day.
Happy Birthday, Tay.
